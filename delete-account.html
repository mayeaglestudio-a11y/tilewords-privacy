Delete your account

Good ol' Tile Words · by May Eagle Studio

You only have an account if you signed in to use the leaderboard. You can delete it whenever you like, in the game or by asking us.

In the game: the quickest way

  1. Open Good ol' Tile Words and tap Leaderboard.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.
  4. If you're asked to sign in once more, use the same account. This is a security check.

It happens straight away.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email mayeaglestudio@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account, and tell us how you signed in: your Play Games gamer name, or the email address of your Google account. We may write back to confirm the request came from you. We'll delete your account within 60 days and let you know when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account.
  • Your leaderboard entry: your in-game name, avatar, best scores and number of games.
  • Every game of yours on the leaderboard, including the moves.

We keep nothing afterwards. There are no backups of the leaderboard, so once deleted, it's gone for good.

What stays on your phone

Your name, avatar, saved game and game history on your phone are not part of your account, and deleting your account doesn't touch them. You can keep playing as before. To remove them as well, uninstall the game, or clear its data in your phone's settings.
